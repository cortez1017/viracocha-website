Our National Organization

Latinos Siempre Unidos

Viracocha at Stevens is a chapter of Lambda Sigma Upsilon (LSU), a national Latino fraternity founded in 1979. Our founders brought that legacy to Stevens on October 26, 2022 — and we've carried it forward ever since.
